01 — Only if it were up to me

I would've stayed.

Zed, I just want you to know na willing akong gawin lahat just so I could keep you in my life and love you the way you deserved to be loved.

Sinasabi ko 'yan not because I want you to change your mind, but because I don't want us to end with me being unable to express my love for you.

I love you. I still do. And kung ako lang sana 'yong masusunod, I would still choose you.

“I would choose you. But I can't choose for you.”

Alam kong minahal mo rin ako, for a brief moment, naramdaman ko 'yon. But you can love me, and want me, and still not want a life with me, you're allowed. I have to respect that. I don't have the right to take that choice away from you just because it breaks my heart.

02 — The moments I regret

I'm sorry.

I'm sorry for all the inconveniences na binigay ko sa'yo, I'm sorry for the moments when I became difficult to understand, for the times I spiraled, overthought, needed reassurance, or let my mind speak louder than my heart.

But please know that none of it came from a place of hate. Never kong ginustong saktan or pahirapan ka. Sometimes I just loved you so much that I didn't know what to do with everything I was feeling. The fear of losing you crept in to my mind every single time and I felt powerless to stop it.

That doesn't erase the inconveniences na dinala ko sa'yo. I'm not asking you to excuse me. I just hope that someday you'll understand where it came from.

I was never trying to make your life harder. I was trying, sometimes badly, not to lose you.

I'll treasure everything.

I don't want the ending to rewrite everything that came before it. Just because it didn't work doesn't mean it's meaningless to me, it just wasn't meant to be.

These are six memories that I keep coming back to. Maybe they're ordinary moments to you now, or something na nakalimutan mo na, but they carry a lot of sentimental value for me.

04 — Why I need to

Kilala ko sarili ko.

I know na I'll spend hours looking for the perfect repost, notes or story that might have changed your mind.

I'll constantly check your socials to see if may bago ka na ba. I'll wonder if you changed your mind. I'll look for tiny signs that maybe you still think about me.

And kada gagawin ko 'yon, I'll be putting myself back at the beginning.

So blocking you isn't punishment. It isn't anger. It's me choosing peace.

I need to stop watching from a distance and hoping for something that you may never want again. I need to give myself the space to actually heal. I can't watch your life in pictures like I used to watch you sleep.

Please don't take the block as hate. I could never make myself hate you just to make leaving easier.
